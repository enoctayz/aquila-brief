Fine-tuning FreyaTTS on Enocta voices

We fine-tuned an open-source Turkish TTS model on voices from our own course content

  • 183M parameters

How FreyaTTS works

  1. Turkish text 92 characters, no phonemes
    to
  2. Text encoder + duration predictor
    to
  3. random noise to
    DiT × 16 noise to speech in 32 steps
    to
  4. AudioVAE2 decodes to 48 kHz audio

Flow matching: the transformer learns to turn random noise into speech, guided by the text.

Experiments

Experiment 1

Single speaker: Mehmet Auf

2.4 h 1,785 clips

  • Best: step 4,000 (10k steps overfit: shaky voice)
  • Stumbles on rare words (64% of words appear once)
  • Effectively voice cloning: one model per person

Experiment 2

Multi-speaker model

~104 h 5 speakers · ~84k clips

  • Added a speaker embedding: one model, many voices
  • Best: step 3,000, our best so far, not yet perfect
