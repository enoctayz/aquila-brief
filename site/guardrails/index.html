Risks we do not carry

  • A learner's browser will not run code the model wrote.
  • Our system prompts do not leave the service.
  • Uploaded content cannot take control of what a learner hears.
  • A podcast cannot quietly drift off its source.
  • A failed check never ships.
  • Our safety does not depend on the model behaving.

Deterministic by design. Fail-closed by default.

No model can be guaranteed never to be misled. We engineer for blast-radius containment: if the model is misled, the result does not reach a learner.
